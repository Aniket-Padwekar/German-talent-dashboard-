    How this was built

    Who is in the data

    Every man the DFB has used in a senior international since 1990, from the German Wikipedia list of German national players. The second group is players born in Germany who play for another nation (at least five caps, born 1975 or later); it is small and only descriptive. Neither group is ranked, and no rating or score is used.

    Which youth club counts

    Last youth club is the German professional club whose youth side the player was in just before turning professional, and gives each player one credit. First pro academy is the first professional club in his youth career. Any academy counts every professional club he passed through, so players count more than once. Amateur clubs are not counted, which is why some players have no academy.

    Where the youth paths come from

    Youth careers come from English Wikipedia and, for players born from about 1985, the Transfermarkt dataset published by dcaribou. Where both exist they name the same last youth club in 92 of 93 cases. Players born before 1985 rely on Wikipedia alone.

    Played abroad

    Taken from each player's senior career on Wikipedia. Loans count. Reserve teams are ignored. "Other Big-5 country" means a club in England, Spain, Italy or France.

    Limits

    Youth lists on Wikipedia are incomplete, so some players have no academy shown. Differences of one or two players between clubs are within noise. Club pins are home stadiums, not academy grounds.
